2. ______ : a sugar that stores energy

4. Why would a plant in a dark closet stop growing?
5. What gas do plants release?
  1. Oxygen
  2. The sun
  3. Chloroplasts
  4. Fungi, bacteria, earthworms
6. ______ : the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy
7. "chloroplast" means the organelle where photosynthesis happens. T   F
8. Where in the cell does photosynthesis occur?
9. What does "glucose" mean?
  1. a sugar that stores energy
  2. water falling as rain, snow, sleet, or hail
  3. the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy
  4. green pigment that absorbs light
Show the answer key
  1. 1-B, 2-A, 3-D, 4-C
  2. glucose
  3. False ("photosynthesis" means the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy.)
  4. No light energy means no photosynthesis, so no glucose is made.
  5. A. Oxygen
  6. photosynthesis
  7. True
  8. Chloroplasts
  9. A. a sugar that stores energy

Key vocabulary

  • photosynthesis: the process plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy
  • chlorophyll: green pigment that absorbs light
  • chloroplast: the organelle where photosynthesis happens
  • glucose: a sugar that stores energy
