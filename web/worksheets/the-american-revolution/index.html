The American Revolution worksheet

After the French and Indian War, Britain taxed the colonies (Stamp Act, Townshend Acts, Tea Act) without colonial representation. This free history worksheet for 5th–8th grade checks understanding with vocabulary, key ideas and real questions, and comes with an answer key.
