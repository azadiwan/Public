Name: ________________________Date: __________

Read each question carefully and answer in complete sentences where needed.

1. Match each word to its meaning.
____ 1. matter
____ 2. melting
____ 3. evaporation
____ 4. condensation
Definitions appear here, shuffled, on the printable.
2. Adding or removing heat causes changes of state: ______, freezing, evaporation, condensation.
3. "evaporation" means liquid to gas. T   F
4. Why do water drops form on a cold glass?
5. Name the three common states of matter.
  1. No light energy means no photosynthesis, so no glucose is made.
  2. It changes from liquid to solid.
  3. Solid, liquid, gas
  4. Chloroplasts
6. ______ is anything that has mass and takes up space.
7. "condensation" means liquid to gas. T   F
8. What happens to water when it freezes?
9. Which state has particles that are closest together?
  1. Solid, liquid, gas
  2. Gas is pushed down; the gas pushes the rocket up.
  3. Energy is lost at each level, so less supports higher levels.
  4. Solid
Show the answer key
  1. 1-C, 2-D, 3-B, 4-A
  2. melting
  3. True
  4. Water vapor in the air condenses on the cold surface.
  5. C. Solid, liquid, gas
  6. matter
  7. False ("condensation" means gas to liquid.)
  8. It changes from liquid to solid.
  9. D. Solid

Key vocabulary

  • matter: anything with mass that takes up space
  • melting: solid to liquid
  • evaporation: liquid to gas
  • condensation: gas to liquid
