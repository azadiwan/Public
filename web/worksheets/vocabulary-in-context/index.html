Vocabulary in context worksheet

Context clues are hints in nearby words: definitions, synonyms, antonyms, examples. This free test prep worksheet for 3rd–8th grade checks understanding with vocabulary, key ideas and real questions, and comes with an answer key.

  3. word part added to the beginning

Show the answer key
  1. 1-D, 2-C, 3-B, 4-A
  2. prefix
  3. True
  4. Very dry
  5. A. Kind, generous
  6. connotation
  7. False ("root" means the core part of a word.)
  8. Wrong or badly
  9. A. a hint in the text about a word's meaning

Key vocabulary

  • context clue: a hint in the text about a word's meaning
  • prefix: word part added to the beginning
  • root: the core part of a word
  • connotation: the feeling a word suggests
