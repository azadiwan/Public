Map skills & geography lesson plan

A ready-to-teach social studies lesson for 2nd–5th grade. Every block is timed, from the hook to the exit ticket, and slides, a worksheet and an answer key are one click away.

Aligned to C3 D2.Geo.2.3-5 · PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, PDF, Word

Hook

You're dropped in a new city with only a paper map. How do you find the library?

Learning objectives

  • Explain maps use a title, compass rose, key/legend, and scale.
  • Apply key vocabulary (compass rose, legend, scale) accurately in speaking and writing.
  • Compare solutions or claims about map skills & geography using evidence.

Lesson outline (45 minutes)

  1. Warm-up & hook · 5 min
    • You're dropped in a new city with only a paper map. How do you find the library?
    • Think–pair–share: jot an answer, discuss with a partner, share out.
    • Record predictions/questions on the class chart.
  2. Mini-lesson (I do) · 11 min
    • Maps use a title, compass rose, key/legend, and scale.
    • Cardinal directions: north, south, east, west.
    • Latitude and longitude lines form a grid to locate places.
  3. Guided practice (We do) · 9 min
    • Model: What does a map legend show?
    • Together: Name the four cardinal directions.
    • Students attempt the next step on whiteboards before you reveal it.
  4. Independent practice (You do) · 11 min
    • 1. What does a map legend show?
    • 2. Name the four cardinal directions.
    • 3. How many continents are there?
  5. Check for understanding · 5 min
    • Quick check: How many continents are there?
    • Whiteboard response or digital poll — scan for misconceptions.
    • Pull a small group for reteach if needed.
  6. Closure & exit ticket · 4 min
    • Exit ticket: If the scale is 1 in = 10 mi, how far is 3 inches?
    • Revisit the hook question — what do we know now?
    • Preview what's next.

Key ideas to teach

  • Maps use a title, compass rose, key/legend, and scale.
  • Cardinal directions: north, south, east, west.
  • Latitude and longitude lines form a grid to locate places.
  • The world has 7 continents and 5 oceans.

Project idea

Driving question: Create a map to guide a new student around our school.

Vocabulary

  • compass rose: shows directions on a map
  • legend: explains map symbols
  • scale: shows real distance
  • latitude: imaginary lines running east–west

Check for understanding

  1. What does a map legend show?
  2. Name the four cardinal directions.
  3. How many continents are there?
