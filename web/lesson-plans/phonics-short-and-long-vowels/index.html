Phonics: short & long vowels lesson plan

A ready-to-teach reading lesson for k–2nd grade. Every block is timed, from the hook to the exit ticket, and slides, a worksheet and an answer key are one click away.

Aligned to CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.RF.1.3 · PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, PDF, Word

Hook

What changes when 'cap' gets a magic e and becomes 'cape'?

Learning objectives

  • I can tell what short & long vowels means.
  • I can show my thinking with a picture, model, or example.
  • I can use new words like "vowel" when I talk about short & long vowels.

Lesson outline (45 minutes)

  1. Warm-up & hook · 5 min
    • What changes when 'cap' gets a magic e and becomes 'cape'?
    • Turn and talk with a partner.
    • Record predictions/questions on the class chart.
  2. Mini-lesson (I do) · 11 min
    • Vowels are a, e, i, o, u (and sometimes y).
    • Short vowels say their sound: a as in cat, e in bed, i in pig, o in hop, u in sun.
    • Long vowels say their name, often with a silent 'magic e' (cake, bike, rope).
  3. Guided practice (We do) · 9 min
    • Model: Is the vowel in 'hat' short or long?
    • Together: Add a silent e to 'kit'. What's the new word?
    • Show me with thumbs up / down.
  4. Independent practice (You do) · 11 min
    • 1. Is the vowel in 'hat' short or long?
    • 2. Add a silent e to 'kit'. What's the new word?
    • 3. Sort: bike, sun, rope, bed
  5. Check for understanding · 5 min
    • Quick check: Sort: bike, sun, rope, bed
    • Fist-to-five: how confident are you?
    • Pull a small group for reteach if needed.
  6. Closure & exit ticket · 4 min
    • Exit ticket: Sort: bike, sun, rope, bed
    • Revisit the hook question — what do we know now?
    • Preview what's next.

Key ideas to teach

  • Vowels are a, e, i, o, u (and sometimes y).
  • Short vowels say their sound: a as in cat, e in bed, i in pig, o in hop, u in sun.
  • Long vowels say their name, often with a silent 'magic e' (cake, bike, rope).
  • Two vowels together often make the first one long (rain, boat).

Project idea

Driving question: Make a class 'magic e' word book with illustrations.

Vocabulary

  • vowel: a, e, i, o, u
  • short vowel: a vowel saying its sound
  • long vowel: a vowel saying its name
  • silent e: an e at the end that makes the vowel long

Check for understanding

  1. Is the vowel in 'hat' short or long?
  2. Add a silent e to 'kit'. What's the new word?
  3. Sort: bike, sun, rope, bed
