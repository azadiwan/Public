Parts of speech lesson plan

A ready-to-teach english / ela lesson for 2nd–5th grade. Every block is timed, from the hook to the exit ticket, and slides, a worksheet and an answer key are one click away.

Aligned to CCSS.ELA-LITERACY.L.3.1 · PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, PDF, Word

Hook

'The happy dog quickly ran home.' Which word is doing the action?

Learning objectives

  • Explain nouns name people, places, things, or ideas.
  • Apply key vocabulary (noun, verb, adjective) accurately in speaking and writing.
  • Compare solutions or claims about parts of speech using evidence.

Lesson outline (45 minutes)

  1. Warm-up & hook · 5 min
    • 'The happy dog quickly ran home.' Which word is doing the action?
    • Think–pair–share: jot an answer, discuss with a partner, share out.
    • Record predictions/questions on the class chart.
  2. Mini-lesson (I do) · 11 min
    • Nouns name people, places, things, or ideas.
    • Verbs show actions or states of being.
    • Adjectives describe nouns; adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
  3. Guided practice (We do) · 9 min
    • Model: Find the verb: 'Maya jumped over the puddle.'
    • Together: Find the adjective: 'The tall giraffe ate leaves.'
    • Students attempt the next step on whiteboards before you reveal it.
  4. Independent practice (You do) · 11 min
    • 1. Find the verb: 'Maya jumped over the puddle.'
    • 2. Find the adjective: 'The tall giraffe ate leaves.'
    • 3. Replace the noun with a pronoun: 'Sam likes pizza.'
  5. Check for understanding · 5 min
    • Quick check: Replace the noun with a pronoun: 'Sam likes pizza.'
    • Whiteboard response or digital poll — scan for misconceptions.
    • Pull a small group for reteach if needed.
  6. Closure & exit ticket · 4 min
    • Exit ticket: Find the adverb: 'She sang loudly.'
    • Revisit the hook question — what do we know now?
    • Preview what's next.

Key ideas to teach

  • Nouns name people, places, things, or ideas.
  • Verbs show actions or states of being.
  • Adjectives describe nouns; adverbs describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
  • Pronouns replace nouns so we don't repeat them.

Project idea

Driving question: Create a 'Mad Libs' style story book for a younger class using parts of speech.

Vocabulary

  • noun: person, place, thing, or idea
  • verb: action or state of being
  • adjective: describes a noun
  • adverb: describes a verb, often ends in -ly

  2. Find the adjective: 'The tall giraffe ate leaves.'
  3. Replace the noun with a pronoun: 'Sam likes pizza.'
