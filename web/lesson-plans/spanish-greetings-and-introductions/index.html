Spanish: greetings & introductions lesson plan

A ready-to-teach world languages lesson for k–8th grade. Every block is timed, from the hook to the exit ticket, and slides, a worksheet and an answer key are one click away.

Aligned to ACTFL Interpersonal Novice · PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, PDF, Word

Hook

You just landed in Mexico City and someone says '¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?' What now?

Learning objectives

  • Explain greetings change by time of day: buenos días, buenas tardes, buenas noches.
  • Apply key vocabulary (hola, ¿Cómo te llamas?, mucho gusto) accurately in speaking and writing.
  • Compare solutions or claims about greetings & introductions using evidence.

Lesson outline (45 minutes)

  1. Warm-up & hook · 5 min
    • You just landed in Mexico City and someone says '¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas?' What now?
    • Think–pair–share: jot an answer, discuss with a partner, share out.
    • Record predictions/questions on the class chart.
  2. Mini-lesson (I do) · 11 min
    • Greetings change by time of day: buenos días, buenas tardes, buenas noches.
    • Introduce yourself: Me llamo… / Soy… ; ask: ¿Cómo te llamas?
    • Ask how someone is: ¿Cómo estás? — Bien, mal, más o menos.
  3. Guided practice (We do) · 9 min
    • Model: How do you say 'good morning'?
    • Together: Answer: ¿Cómo te llamas?
    • Students attempt the next step on whiteboards before you reveal it.
  4. Independent practice (You do) · 11 min
    • 1. How do you say 'good morning'?
    • 2. Answer: ¿Cómo te llamas?
    • 3. When do you use 'usted'?
  5. Check for understanding · 5 min
    • Quick check: When do you use 'usted'?
    • Whiteboard response or digital poll — scan for misconceptions.
    • Pull a small group for reteach if needed.
  6. Closure & exit ticket · 4 min
    • Exit ticket: When do you use 'usted'?
    • Revisit the hook question — what do we know now?
    • Preview what's next.

Key ideas to teach

  • Greetings change by time of day: buenos días, buenas tardes, buenas noches.
  • Introduce yourself: Me llamo… / Soy… ; ask: ¿Cómo te llamas?
  • Ask how someone is: ¿Cómo estás? — Bien, mal, más o menos.
  • Tú is informal; usted is formal (adults, strangers).

Project idea

Driving question: Record a short video introducing yourself to a pen pal class in a Spanish-speaking country.

Vocabulary

  • hola: hello
  • ¿Cómo te llamas?: What's your name?
  • mucho gusto: nice to meet you
  • adiós: goodbye

Check for understanding

  2. Answer: ¿Cómo te llamas?
  3. When do you use 'usted'?
