Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

LaunchPoint is built so teachers can plan without handing over personal data. Here's exactly what happens to information on the site.

What stays on your device

Your lessons, worksheets, library and settings are saved in your own browser (local storage). We don't have a copy. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What is sent to our servers

  • Lesson and worksheet requests: when you generate a full lesson plan, custom worksheet or rewrite, the topic, grade, settings and any text you provide are sent to our server and to our content-generation provider, Anthropic, to produce the result. We don't use this to identify you, and we don't sell it.
  • Uploaded files for "Upload & improve" are read in your browser. Only the extracted text is sent, and only if you ask for a rewrite.
  • Analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies or track you across other sites.

Payments

If you buy Pro, Stripe processes the payment and stores your email, name and card details under Stripe's privacy policy. We can see your email, plan and the last 4 digits of your card so we can confirm and restore your subscription. We never see your full card number.

Student data

LaunchPoint never asks for student information. Please don't enter students' names or other personal details into the tool.

Children

LaunchPoint is for adults who teach. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

Your choices

You can delete your saved work at any time by clearing your browser data. To ask about or delete payment records, email us.
