Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Every worksheet on LaunchPoint is free to make, print and share with your class, with answer keys included. No sign-up needed.

How do I make a whole winter break packet?

Click your grade's button. It builds a mixed review worksheet instantly. Hit “New numbers” to make more pages, then download each as a PDF and staple them together. Every page gets fresh problems, so you can make different versions for different students.

Do you have ready-made packets?

Yes. Our print-and-go Winter Break Math Review packs for 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th grade (20 sheets each, with answer keys) are on our Teachers Pay Teachers store.
