The voice is a generated narrator voice (Kokoro, an open-source voice licensed for commercial use). The scripts below are exactly what it says.

## How to post (about 3 minutes per video)

1. Send the .mp4 to your phone (AirDrop, Google Drive, or email it to yourself) and save it to your camera roll.
2. In TikTok, tap **+ › Upload** and pick the video. **Keep the original sound on**, since that's the voiceover.
3. Optional: add quiet background music from TikTok's **Commercial Music Library** and set it to about 10–15% volume so the voice stays clear.
4. Turn on **"AI-generated content"** under More options, because the narrator voice is generated. It's TikTok's rule for synthetic voices and keeps the account in good standing.
5. Paste the caption and hashtags below, set the cover to the first frame (the hook card), and post.

**Want your own voice instead?** Lower the original sound to 0, tap **Voiceover**, and read the script while the video plays. A real founder's voice often gets more trust and comments.

**Best times to post for teachers:** weekdays 6–8 pm and Sunday 4–8 pm (Sunday-night planning). Post one video every 2–3 days, not all at once.

**Account tip:** Use a TikTok **Business account** named @launchpointedu (or similar). Put `launchpointeducation.com` in the bio: "Free K–8 lesson plans, slides & worksheets in minutes 🚀".

## Honesty notes (keeps the account safe)

- These are real screen recordings of the actual site. Don't edit them to claim speeds or features the site doesn't have.
- With full lesson plan generation turned on, building a lesson takes about 20–40 seconds on the live site. The videos cut straight to the result, which is normal for demos, but don't add a caption saying "instantly" or "in 5 seconds".
- Keep the "AI-generated content" label on while using the built-in narrator voice. If you replace it with your own voice, you can turn the label off.
- Don't present anyone as a teacher who uses the product unless they're a real teacher who agreed to it.

## What to watch after posting

- **Views and average watch time:** if people drop off in the first 2 seconds, test a new hook card.
- **Profile visits and link clicks:** this is what actually matters.
- **Comments:** reply to every teacher question within a day. Replies with video are great follow-up content.
