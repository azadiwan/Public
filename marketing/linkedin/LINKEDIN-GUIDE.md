# LaunchPoint Education: LinkedIn activity kit

Use this kit to post on LinkedIn regularly for four weeks without having to think up posts each time. It covers:

1. **Profile fixes.** A 20-minute, one-time setup so people who see your posts can tell what you do.
2. **12 ready-to-post drafts.** Three a week for four weeks. Copy one, paste it, post it.
3. **A 15-minute daily engagement routine.** On LinkedIn, comments often reach more people than your own posts do.
4. **What to track** so you know what's working.

Anything in `[brackets]` is something only you know (your background, a real number, a real story). Fill it in or delete that line. **Don't post a bracket as-is, and don't make up numbers or stories.**

## 2. Posting plan

**Rhythm:** 3 posts a week (Tue, Wed, Thu are strongest on LinkedIn), around **7:30–8:30 am** or **12–1 pm** your time.

**Rules that help reach:**
- The first 2 lines are the hook. They're all that shows before "…see more."
- Keep the link **out of the post body**. Put `launchpointeducation.com` in the first comment, or write it as plain text without `https://`.
- End with a question so people comment.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour.
- 3–5 hashtags max.

### Post 4: Teaching tip (value, no pitch)

> Almost every strong lesson I've studied has the same 4 parts:
>
> 1️⃣ A hook (2–5 min): a question, image or puzzle that makes students *want* the answer
> 2️⃣ A mini-lesson (10–15 min): one idea, modeled clearly
> 3️⃣ Practice (15–20 min): students do the thinking, not the teacher
> 4️⃣ An exit ticket (3–5 min): one question that tells you who got it
>
> The part most often skipped? The exit ticket. It's also the one that makes tomorrow's lesson better.
>
> Which part do you find hardest to fit in?
>

## 3. Daily engagement routine (15 minutes)

LinkedIn rewards people who show up in other people's comments, not just their own feed.

- **5 min:** Reply to every comment on your posts. Ask a follow-up question back.
- **7 min:** Leave 3–5 *thoughtful* comments (2+ sentences, adding something) on posts from teachers, principals, edtech founders and education writers. Search `#teachers`, `#edtech`, `#lessonplanning`.
- **3 min:** Send 5 connection requests with a short note, e.g. *"Hi [name], I'm building a free lesson-planning tool for K–8 teachers and enjoyed your post on [topic]. Would love to connect."*

**Who to connect with:** K–8 teachers, instructional coaches, curriculum directors, principals, teacher-prep faculty, and edtech founders and investors.

## 4. What to track (weekly, 5 minutes)

| Metric | Where | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impressions per post | Post analytics | Which hooks get read |
| Comments | Post | Shows real interest; LinkedIn boosts posts that get them |
| Profile views | Me → Analytics | Whether posts make people curious about you |
| Followers / connections | Me → Analytics | Long-term reach |
| Site visits from LinkedIn | Vercel Analytics (referrer = linkedin.com) | This is the number that actually matters |

After 2 weeks, write more posts like your top 2 and drop the format that did worst.

## Honesty notes

- Only post real numbers, real stories and real feedback. The brackets are there for that reason.
- Don't call anyone a LaunchPoint user or quote a teacher unless they're real and said yes.
- The demo videos use a generated narrator voice. Mention it if asked, or re-record with your own voice.
- Don't say "instant" or "in seconds". Full AI builds take about 20–40 seconds.
