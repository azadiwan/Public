# LaunchPoint Education: marketing & revenue plan

**Goal:** turn launchpointeducation.com into a business that makes money, starting within 30 days, with costs kept near zero until something proves it works.

## How the money comes in

| # | Revenue stream | Status | Who acts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Teachers Pay Teachers packs.** Sell worksheet packs where teachers already shop. Every pack advertises the site. | **6 packs ready** in `marketing/tpt/` | You open the store and upload (≈45 min). I make new packs weekly. |
| 2 | **Pro subscription ($9/mo or $59/yr).** Unlimited custom-written lessons and worksheets for any topic, leveled versions, cloud library. The free tier stays generous and drives sign-ups. | Needs payments + accounts | You create a Stripe account. I build checkout, sign-in and the Pro gate. |
| 3 | **School & district licenses ($500–$2,500/yr per school).** Shared libraries, training, invoices. | After 1–2 pilot schools | You take the meetings; I make the deck, one-pager, emails and pilot plan. |

## 90-day roadmap

**Days 1–14: launch the basics**
- Merge the search pages, turn on Vercel Analytics, submit the sitemap to Google Search Console.
- Open the TpT store; list 1 free and 5 paid packs.
- Post the 3 TikTok videos; set up a Pinterest business account with 5 boards (Math Worksheets by grade, Lesson Plans, Science, ELA, Teacher Tips).
- Add the email sign-up box to the site ("Get a free worksheet pack every week").

**Days 15–45: turn on revenue**
- Stripe checkout + accounts + Pro gate (stream 2).
- Weekly: 2 new TpT packs, 2–3 short videos, 20–30 Pinterest pins, 1 newsletter, 1 teacher-group post.
- Seasonal packs timed to the school calendar (winter break, testing season, back to school).

**Days 46–90: scale what works**
- Double down on the channel with the cheapest sign-ups; drop the weakest.
- Optional small paid test ($100–300/mo) on Pinterest ads and Google search ads, sent to the best-converting landing pages.
- 1–2 pilot schools for the school plan.

## Weekly scorecard (what we track)
- Site visitors and top landing pages (Vercel Analytics)
- Lessons and worksheets created on the site
- Email sign-ups
- TpT views, downloads, sales, followers
- Pro subscribers and monthly revenue (once live)

Targets get set after 2–4 weeks of real numbers. Guessing them before there's data would be made up.

## Rules we follow
- Honest claims only: real screen recordings, no fake testimonials or fake teachers, label generated voices where platforms require it.
- No spending money or sending emails without your OK.
- Free tier stays genuinely useful. It's the engine of word of mouth.
