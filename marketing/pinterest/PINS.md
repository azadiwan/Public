**Profile:** https://www.pinterest.com/launchpointedu/

Pinterest is where teachers search for classroom resources, and a pin keeps bringing visitors for months, unlike a TikTok. Each pin below is 1000×1500 (the size Pinterest recommends). Each one links to a free page on the site, with tracking tags so Vercel Analytics shows which pins bring visitors (look for `utm_source=pinterest` in the dashboard).

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes)
1. Go to **business.pinterest.com** and choose **Create a free business account**. Use hello@launchpointeducation.com.
2. Name: **LaunchPoint Education**. Website: `launchpointeducation.com`. Description: *Free K–8 lesson plans, slides and printable worksheets with answer keys. Plan tomorrow's lesson in minutes.*
3. **Claim your website:** Settings › Claimed accounts › Website. Pinterest gives you an HTML tag or a TXT record. **Paste it to me** and I'll add it, the same way we did for Google.
4. Create these 5 boards:
   - **Math Worksheets by Grade**
   - **Free Lesson Plans**
   - **Science Lessons**
   - **ELA & Reading**
   - **Teacher Planning Tips**

## Posting plan
Post **2 pins a day**, or use Pinterest's built-in scheduler (**Create › Pin › Publish later**) to load all 10 at once over 5 days. Steady daily posting does better than posting everything at once.
